## What I am agreeing to

I understand that this is an experimental student project, not a production attendance or identity system. Participation is voluntary. Choosing not to participate or withdrawing will not affect my grades, access to classes, or relationships with the university. A manual attendance alternative is available.

The team will explain the process and answer my questions. I may stop at any time without giving a reason. I will not be asked to remove religious clothing or disclose demographic information as a condition of participation.

The app processes face photographs in memory and stores encrypted face embeddings, not raw enrolment photographs. Embeddings are biometric data and are not anonymous. The app may retain encrypted small review crops for up to 24 hours while operating, or until the teacher resolves the item. I can delete my embeddings and revoke application consent from my student account.

The **offline evaluation dataset is separate**. If I consent below, the team may save photographs in an encrypted, access-controlled dataset to evaluate recognition, lighting, distance, pose and spoof resistance. These photographs will not be publicly released or included in the hackathon submission. Only named team members approved by the organiser will access them. Identity mapping and this signed form will be kept separately.

My face will not be used to train an unrelated model, to infer sensitive traits, or for disciplinary decisions. Aggregate performance results may be shown to judges. Public use of identifiable photos or recordings requires an additional, specific permission and is not included here.

## Separate choices (tick only what you agree to)

- [ ] I consent to application enrolment, encrypted embeddings and temporary review processing.
- [ ] I consent to offline evaluation photos in the listed lighting, pose and distance conditions.
- [ ] I consent to appearing in evaluation group photographs with other consenting volunteers.
- [ ] I consent to the team printing or displaying my portrait to collect print/screen spoof evaluation shots. Copies will be destroyed/deleted by the scheduled deletion date.
- [ ] I consent to short webcam bursts for blink/head-turn challenge evaluation.

I understand the risks of biometric data exposure, recognition errors and spoofing. The team will use access controls and encryption, but cannot promise zero risk. Photos will be deleted by the date recorded above, including controlled copies, and deletion will be recorded. Application attendance/audit records may remain under the published retention policy without face embeddings. Published anonymous aggregates cannot necessarily be retracted.

To withdraw offline evaluation data, I can contact the organiser and provide my volunteer ID. The organiser will locate and remove my enrolment, probe and spoof files and remove or re-collect group photos that contain me. My request will be acknowledged, and the team will confirm deletion from controlled working copies and backups according to the stated deletion schedule.
